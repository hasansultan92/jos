Exercise 1

Question 1. Do you have to do anything else to ensure that this I/O privilege 
setting is saved and restored properly when you subsequently switch from one 
environment to another? Why?

No, we do not have to do anything else to ensure that this I/O privilege
setting is saved and restored properly. This is because IO privilege is 
stored in the eflags register (tf_eflags), which is part of the trap frame 
(env_tf)--which will be automatically be saved and restored when switching 
from one environment to another.
