Exercise 1

Question 1. Do you have to do anything else to ensure that this I/O privilege 
setting is saved and restored properly when you subsequently switch from one 
environment to another? Why?

No, we do not have to do anything else to ensure that this I/O privelege
setting is saved and restored properly. This is because the IO privelege is 
stored in the eflags register--so it will be automatically restored and 
restored when subsequently switching from one environment to another.
